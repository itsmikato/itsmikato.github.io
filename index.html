About

Mikato is Michael Kittel, a computer scientist and designer based in Barcelona, always somewhere in between art and technology. I design and build interactive things – interfaces, games, audio tools and generative art – from the first sketch to working code.

Two years of product design at FH Münster, a B.Sc. in computer science at the University of Münster with a thesis on the geometry of neural networks, and an Erasmus year at the Universitat de Barcelona. I work in Python, C++, TypeScript and React, Godot and JUCE, and draw in Illustrator. German, English and Spanish, Catalan in progress.

I'm looking for a role as design engineer or creative developer in Barcelona – a studio or product team where design and code meet. Collaborations and commissions are welcome too.
