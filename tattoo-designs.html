Tattoo Designs

2025 · Fine-line tattoo designs

Fine-line tattoo designs, most of them from the Flow set. Plants grow, curl and droop from a single horizon line: arching stems ending in heart-shaped leaves, a spiralling fern, flowers in slender vases. Some are left as pure linework, others get a soft wash of green or violet. Alongside them: small vessels and a snail, feathery leaves and a branching sprig, a finished plant, and the nudibranch reworked as a tattoo.
