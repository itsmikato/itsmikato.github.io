Space Invaders

2024 · Terminal game – C++, ncurses – team project with Max Feige and Moritz Nauen

A take on the arcade classic that runs entirely in the terminal. Arrow keys move, space shoots; the alien block marches on, fires back, and every level gets faster. Upgrades drop now and then, and score, lives and level sit under the field. Built in C++ with ncurses at university, with the game logic and the console view kept apart and unit tests for the logic.
