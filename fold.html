Fold

2026 · Puzzle game for iPhone and the browser, in progress – Swift, SwiftUI, JavaScript, pixel art

A puzzle game about folding a sheet of paper over itself – work in progress. You can play it right here in the browser. You play a paper boat on a sheet, and the goal is a sheet of coloured paper. Fold the sheet and the top layer wins: holes become windows onto the layer below, pins stop a layer from lifting. Each level awards up to three pinwheels for using few folds, and the islands – leaf, wave, ember, sky – each have their own colours, map and soundtrack. The look is deliberately its own: one-bit pixel art in ink and paper, a pixel font and chunky buttons instead of the style of this site. It is a native iPhone app written in SwiftUI, with a browser version that follows it.

Case study

Role

Solo project – game design, level design, pixel art, music, Swift

Tools

Puzzle game for iPhone and the browser, in progress – Swift, SwiftUI, JavaScript, pixel art

The challenge

Make a puzzle where one simple move – folding the sheet over itself – stays interesting for a hundred levels, and where every level can really be solved.

Decisions

  1. Few rules that combine

    The top layer wins, holes are windows and pins block lifting. Each rule is easy to read on its own; the puzzles come from how they meet.

  2. Levels proven, not hoped for

    A script checks that every level is solvable and searches for new ones, and a rating pass sorts them by difficulty, so the order teaches the game step by step.

  3. Native, with its own look

    I built it as a real SwiftUI app instead of a web wrapper, with its own pixel font, themed islands and soundtracks composed by script – coherent in itself, not a copy of my portfolio.

Result

A playable iPhone game with several islands of levels, an intro and endings, tested on my own phone.
