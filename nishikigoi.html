Nishikigoi

2022 · Poster series – Illustrator, InDesign

錦鯉 – “brocaded carp”, the Japanese name for ornamental koi. A poster series built around a single hand-drawn koi, its scales and fins worked into bold black patterns. The same fish is repeated across five colourways (mint, coral, sand, navy and teal), with the kanji stacked in fading rows at opposite corners.
