Good Llama

2026 · Hand drawing, HTML & CSS 3D, AI animation

Good morning, good llama. A hand-drawn llama – black, with fine outlines and patches of deep blue – inside a ring of teal lettering, and it looks at you: move the mouse anywhere on the page and it turns its head to follow. There is only the one drawing, seen from the front, so the turn is built from depth: the ears sit furthest back, the eyes in the middle and the snout closest, and as the head turns, the nearer parts swing further. Left alone, it sways and blinks. The drawing was first made as a gift. Before the live version came a five-second turnaround loop animated with AI, in a vertical 9:16 phone format.
