Papilionidae

2026 · Poster series – Illustrator, Python

Three swallowtails, each settling right where two sweeping curves meet. It began in 2024 with Agamemnon – Graphium agamemnon, the tailed jay – drawn flat in Illustrator, its spots cut out of mint wings so the violet behind shows through, with a shifted violet copy as its shadow. Apollo and Ulysses follow the same rules: traced from two public-domain plates (an old Apollo illustration and Cramer & Stoll's Papilio ulysses from 1779), smoothed into flat shapes with a small Python script and given a pair of colours of their own – cream and coral for the Apollo's red eyespots, pale sky and deep blue for the Ulysses.
