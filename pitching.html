Pitching

2026 · Singing practice app – web app and iPhone app, TypeScript, Swift

An app that teaches you to sing in tune, one short melody at a time. It plays a melody in a soft, voice-like tone and shows it as a score of pastel fields; then it's your turn, and your voice becomes an ink line over the notes. Six levels go from holding one note to whole songs, and each melody earns up to three dots – across days, not in one sitting. Built on what voice science says actually helps, and in the same look as the rest of my work: paper, ink and flat colour instead of a music app's game screens.

Try it – works best on a phone ↗︎

Case study

Role

Solo project – research, interaction design, visual design, code (web and iOS)

Tools

Singing practice app – web app and iPhone app, TypeScript, Swift

The challenge

Pitch Coach showed me how far off I was, but not how to get better. I wanted a practice app I'd open every day with a guitar on my lap – one that teaches, instead of just measuring, and doesn't look like a game.

Decisions

  1. Built on research, not guesswork

    Before designing the levels I went through the studies on how adults learn to sing in tune. The app follows them: hear it, then sing it back; a live line that fades level by level, because constant help stops you hearing yourself; tests without any help; and a pass band that starts wide (±50 cents) and tightens.

  2. Progress you earn across days

    A melody's first dot comes from practice, the second from a test on another day, the third from one in another key. Melodies come back for review after 1, 3 and 7 days, and a level opens only once the one before is done.

  3. Calm instead of gamified

    No confetti, streaks or badges: dots, a week of flat fields, and one honest sentence after each take – including how far untrained and trained singers usually are, so a number means something.

Result

An installable web app and a native iPhone app (SwiftUI) with the same 26 melodies, sharing one pitch detector ported from Pitch Coach's C++. Everything runs on the phone – nothing is recorded or sent.
