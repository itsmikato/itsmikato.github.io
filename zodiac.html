Zodiac

2026 · Video game in progress – Godot 4, Blender

A monster-collecting game on an island of cut paper and ink – work in progress. You travel from region to region, meet and fight the zodimon that live there and win the twelve signs of the zodiac from their guardians. Combat happens in real time, right in the overworld: dodge, parry, switch zodimon mid-fight. Every region is one flat field of colour taken from the koi posters, with paper trees, pen contours and ink marks on the ground; the menus are set in the same type as this site. Every zodimon is built in Blender by script – modelled, rigged, animated and painted with ink patterns – and the game is written in GDScript.
