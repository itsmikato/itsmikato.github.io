Better Now than Never

2026 · Song and cover – Ableton Live, an Illustrator script

A song by Mikato, with a cover drawn by code. Not released yet – here is a 44-second instrumental preview. Written and produced in Ableton Live at 120 BPM, in A minor; some of the bass lines were generated with small Python scripts. The cover is drawn by a script in Illustrator: thin lines in pastel rainbow colours flow across soft pink and bend around a pale sun. Before it came a sunset under the German working title, a title graphic of soft colour blobs, and the same design on deep navy.
