Symbios

2026 · Synthesizer plugin – C++, JUCE (AU, VST3, standalone)

A synthesizer that runs as a plugin in any music program. Three oscillators with their own wave, octave and tuning feed a mixer with noise, then a ladder filter with drive and key tracking. A filter envelope and a loudness envelope shape every note, an LFO can move pitch and filter, and it plays mono with glide or polyphonic, with reverb at the end. Written in C++ with the JUCE framework, one colour per section of the signal path.
