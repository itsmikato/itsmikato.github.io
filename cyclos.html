Cyclos

2022 · Pop-up book – team project with Felix Gutendorf

A pop-up book about the cycles of life and how nature renews itself. Made at the Münster School of Design, FH Münster, in summer 2022. Each spread opens into layered, cut paper in bold, flat colours – an eye, a spiral, a sprout pushing up, a cluster of eggs – behind a cover with a cut-out sun. The book was built up week by week from white paper tests and colour studies, and filmed at the end.
