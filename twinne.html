Twinne

2022 · Product design – team project with Johannes Benedix, Sophie Kothé and Margo Rindle

A coat hanger in two halves, held together in the middle by magnets. Made in the product design course “the making of things” at the Münster School of Design, FH Münster, winter semester 2022/23. A loop of recycled climbing rope, the old sheath with its core taken out, links the two halves and replaces the hook, so clothes can hang wherever and however they're needed. It comes in two woods: robust beech multiplex and a lighter bamboo plywood. Milled, sanded, oiled and laser-cut in the university workshops, with logo, screen-printed shirts, posters and a short film along the way.
